# User Interviews

## Interview 1

### User
Student developer using ChatGPT and Cursor.

### Pain Point
Paying for multiple AI tools without understanding whether all subscriptions are necessary.

### Feedback
Wanted a simple dashboard showing:
- overlapping subscriptions
- cheaper alternatives
- possible savings

---

## Interview 2

### User
Startup founder managing a small engineering team.

### Pain Point
Difficult to track AI spending across multiple employees and tools.

### Feedback
Liked the idea of:
- centralized AI spend visibility
- optimization recommendations
- infrastructure credits

---

## Interview 3

### User
Freelance developer using GitHub Copilot and Claude.

### Pain Point
Unclear whether premium plans provided enough additional value.

### Feedback
Preferred concise recommendations and realistic savings estimates.

---

## Insights

Common patterns discovered:
- users want transparency in AI costs
- most users do not track AI subscriptions properly
- teams often overpay due to overlapping tools
- users prefer actionable recommendations over complex analytics
