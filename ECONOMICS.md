# Economics

## Lead Value Estimation

A startup spending heavily on AI tools could save hundreds or thousands of dollars yearly through optimization and infrastructure credits.

Estimated converted lead value:
- $300–$2000 depending on company size and AI usage.

---

## Customer Acquisition Cost

Estimated CAC by channel:

- Product Hunt: Low CAC due to organic discovery
- Twitter/X: Medium CAC through founder engagement
- LinkedIn posts: Low CAC
- Startup communities: Very low CAC

Estimated blended CAC:
- Approximately $10–$50 per qualified lead.

---

## Conversion Funnel

Estimated funnel:
- 100 audit users
- 20 email captures
- 5 consultation calls
- 1 converted customer

---

## Profitability

The tool becomes profitable if:
- consultation conversion remains above 5%
- average customer value exceeds acquisition cost

## Growth Potential

If the platform reaches:
- 10,000 monthly audits

the business could generate significant recurring infrastructure revenue.
