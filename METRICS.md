# Metrics

## North Star Metric

Completed AI audits per week.

This directly measures:
- user engagement
- product usefulness
- lead generation potential

---

## Input Metrics

### 1. Audit Completion Rate
Measures how many users finish the audit process.

### 2. Email Capture Rate
Measures how many users submit contact information after receiving value.

### 3. Consultation Requests
Measures high-intent users interested in deeper optimization.

---

## Product Instrumentation

The first events to track:
- audit started
- audit completed
- email submitted
- consultation CTA clicked

---

## Success Indicators

Positive early signals:
- users sharing audit screenshots
- repeat usage
- strong email capture conversion

---

## Pivot Trigger

If audit completion remains low despite traffic growth, the onboarding flow or recommendation quality would need improvement.
